At CMB | The Business Flow App (accessible via cmb.cmfilings.com), the privacy and security of our users and their respective clients are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by CMB and how we use it to provide our clinic and administration management services.

By using our application, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

gpp_good Zero Third-Party Data Sharing Guarantee

We strictly adhere to a zero data-sharing policy. We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information, clinic data, financial records, or patient details with any third-party organizations, advertisers, or external services under any circumstances. Your data belongs to you and remains entirely confidential within your secured workspace.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services effectively, we collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register an account, manage your clinic, or input operational data into the system. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and administrative credentials.
  • Clinic & Operational Data: Branch details, therapist schedules, daily attendance, and financial transactions (invoices, advances, and OP balances).
  • Patient Data: Information entered into the CRM, including patient names, contact numbers, medical histories/notes, and appointment logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for the operational functionality of your specific workspace. We use your data to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the CMB Business Flow platform.
  • Process clinical appointments, schedules, and financial transactions.
  • Generate automated invoices, payment receipts (UPI QRs), and business reports.
  • Authenticate user access and enforce role-based security (e.g., preventing unauthorized staff from viewing out-of-branch data).
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust, industry-standard security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data is securely stored using enterprise-grade cloud databases (Firebase/Firestore) with strict security rules enforcing authentication and authorization per user role.

4. Log Files & Analytics

CMB follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they use the application. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is strictly for analyzing system performance, administering the site, and preventing fraudulent activity.

5. Your Data Rights

As the sole owner of your workspace data, you retain the right to:

  • Access, modify, or update your clinic and patient records at any time.
  • Export your data (via the Export & Backup module) for your own offline archival purposes.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and associated records by contacting your system administrator or CMB support.
