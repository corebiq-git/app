Module Export (CSV/Excel)

Download specific tables directly to your device. Staff accounts will only export data related to their assigned branch.

info
Leave the date fields empty to export all available records for the selected module. Date filters apply to the 'Date' or 'Creation Date' depending on the module.

Full System Backup (JSON)

Download a complete snapshot of all databases. This is useful for migrating data or keeping secure offline records.

warning
This action will fetch the entire database across all branches. Please ensure you are on a stable internet connection as this may take a moment.
